# Passive Cooling Safety Helmet Liner

A dual phase thermal management system engineered to mitigate heat stress for workers and riders operating in extreme temperatures.

---

## Key Features & Specifications

* **Microclimate Temperature Reduction:** Lowers internal helmet temperature by **7°C to 8°C**
* **Mass Budget:** **~360 grams extra** (lightweight design balanced for comfort and structural stability)
* **Target Manufacturing Cost:** **Under 60 AED** (cost effective for large scale adoption)
* **Safety Integration:** Integrates seamlessly inside standard hard hats without altering structural integrity

---

## Thermal Management Architecture

The liner operates using a dual layer cooling system:

1. **Primary Layer (Sodium Polyacrylate Hydrogel):**
   * Continuously absorbs perspiration
   * Utilizes active evaporative cooling to pull heat directly away from the head

2. **Secondary Layer (Paraffin Based PCMs):**
   * Undergoes an endothermic phase transition when ambient heat reaches peak operating thresholds
   * Absorbs excess thermal energy to maintain a steady microclimate equilibrium
